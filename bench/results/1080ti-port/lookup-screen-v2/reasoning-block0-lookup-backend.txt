### Step 1: Understand the Problem

You want to find the **sum of all integers from 1 to 100**. That is:

$$
1 + 2 + 3 + \dots + 99 + 100
$$

This is a **finite arithmetic series**.

---

### Step 2: Use the Formula for the Sum of an Arithmetic Series

The general formula for the sum of the first **n** positive integers is:

$$
S_n = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}
$$

Where:
- $ n $ is the number of terms (in this case, 100),
- $ S_n $ is the sum of the first $ n $ positive integers.
