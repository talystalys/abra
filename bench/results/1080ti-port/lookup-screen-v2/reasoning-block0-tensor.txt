### **Step 1: Understand the Problem**

We want to find the **sum of all integers from 1 to 100**.

That is:

$$
1 + 2 + 3 + \dots + 99 + 100
$$

We can write this as:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{100} i
$$

---

### **Step 2: Use the Formula for the Sum of the First n Integers**

There is a well-known formula for the sum of the first **n** positive integers:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}
$$

This formula is derived from the **arithmetic series**.
